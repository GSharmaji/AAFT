Meta just handed performance marketers a sharper lever — and most people will ignore it. 🎯

There are 4 ways to tell Meta what "good" actually means. Pick the wrong one and you optimise for the wrong outcome:

📈 Highest Volume → most conversions your budget can buy. Great for scaling winners. Careful: it chases volume, not value.

🎯 Cost Per Result → holds a target CPA while it scales. Set the cap too low and delivery stalls.

💰 ROAS Goal (NEW) → optimises for return, prioritising higher-value purchases — not just more orders.
⚠ The catch: it's only available on NEW ad sets. You can't flip an existing one — duplicate and test.

🎛️ Bid Cap → hard control on your max bid. Powerful, but needs real auction data and active management.

The biggest takeaway?
The bid strategy is a lever, not a setting. Volume, cost, or profit — match it to the goal.

If you run e-commerce or value-based campaigns, ROAS Goal is worth a test this week.
