What a shopper gets

  • One piece, every option. Metal, stone, shape and size change on the spot, so no option is left to imagination.
  • Honest scale. Real millimetres next to every carat weight.
  • Their own skin tone. Yellow gold and platinum read very differently on different skin.
  • Six shots in a fixed order. Hero, profile, top, detail, tone, on model.
  • The piece is never redrawn. The same exact 3D piece sits on every model, so what is shown is what ships.
