HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
- You post from your personal profile, so your name/photo already show. The
  content stays brand-neutral on purpose — no company, no client, no contact
  block. Just you, being useful (and a little funny).
- Cadence: 3 posts/week (Mon / Wed / Fri) → ~3.5 weeks of content.
- For each post below you get: a Title, the Hook, the full Caption (copy-paste),
  Hashtags, a ready-to-paste Gemini image prompt, and Alt text.
- Reply to every comment in the first 60 minutes. That single habit does more
  for reach than any hashtag.
- Rotate the color variant (dark / cream / lime) noted on each post so your feed
  grid looks like a designed set, not ten identical tiles.

SWAP TIPS (for when you're in Gemini)
=====================================================================
- Change the highlighted word: pick the single most surprising or punchy word in
  the headline — that's the one that goes in the lime box. One box per card only.
- Switch the mood: same prompt, just change "Variant:" to DARK / CREAM / LIME.
  Keep roughly a 5 dark / 3 cream / 2 lime mix across the ten for a clean grid.
- Want a subtle personal mark? Add to any prompt: "Small in the top-left corner,
  set your initials 'GS' in a heavy sans, with a lime dot after them." (Optional
  — your profile already carries your name.)
- If a headline looks cramped, tell Gemini: "shorten line length, wrap the
  headline onto 2–3 lines, keep the highlight box on one word."
- Keep the aspect ratio at 4:5 (1080x1350) — it takes the most vertical space in
  the LinkedIn feed.
