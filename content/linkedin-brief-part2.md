 SECTION 2 — HOOK CHEAT SHEET  (write your own, forever)
#####################################################################
The first line decides everything. On LinkedIn, only the first ~2 lines show
before "…see more." Earn the click. Steal these formulas:

1. THE CALLED-OUT BELIEF
   "[Common belief] is wrong. Here's what actually happens."
   e.g. "'More followers = more sales' is a myth. Here's the math."

2. THE CONFESSION
   "I used to [embarrassing thing]. Here's what changed."
   e.g. "I used to send 400-word cold emails. Nobody replied. Obviously."

3. THE ENEMY
   Name the thing your audience quietly hates.
   e.g. "'Let's just boost the post' has ruined more budgets than any recession."

4. THE NUMBER + TWIST
   "[Surprising number]. And [counterintuitive takeaway]."
   e.g. "90% of your homepage goes unread. The 10% that's read is all headline."

5. THE ANALOGY
   Compare a work idea to something mundane and funny.
   e.g. "Chasing every trend is cereal for dinner. Fun for a week."

6. THE 'IF YOU… THEN' CALL-OUT
   "If you're [doing X], you're probably [losing Y]."
   e.g. "If you reply to leads 'when you get a chance,' you're losing most of them."

7. THE UNCOMFORTABLE TRUTH
   "Nobody wants to say this, but…"
   e.g. "Nobody wants to say this, but your logo isn't your problem."

8. THE ONE-LINE STORY
   Start mid-scene. No windup.
   e.g. "A client asked me to 'make it go viral.' So I asked one question…"

9. THE LIST TEASE
   "[N] things I wish I knew before [thing]. #3 still stings."

10. THE QUESTION FLIP
    Ask the thing they're secretly worried about.
    e.g. "Be honest: could a stranger tell what you do in one sentence?"

RULES OF THUMB
- Line 1 = curiosity or a nerve. Line 2 = keep the tension. Never explain yet.
- Short lines. Lots of white space. One idea per post.
- End with ONE question — make it easy and a little fun to answer.
- Write the hook LAST, after you know the point. Then cut it in half.

 SECTION 3 — POSTING CALENDAR (India / IST)
#####################################################################
BEST WINDOWS FOR AN INDIA AUDIENCE (LinkedIn)
- Prime: Tue / Wed / Thu, 8:30–10:00 AM IST (the commute + first-coffee scroll).
- Strong second: 5:30–7:00 PM IST (wind-down scroll).
- Decent: ~1:00 PM IST (lunch).
- Avoid: Fri evening, Sat, Sun morning. Mon is okay but not your best material.
- Post ONE thing per day, max. Then go reply to comments for the next hour —
  that first-hour engagement is what the algorithm actually rewards.

  out or crowd your own reach.

  post ("Someone asked me X — here's the longer answer"). Free, high-performing,
  and it rewards the people already engaging with you.
