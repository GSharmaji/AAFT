Scaling isn't "spend more." It's "refresh before it dies."

=====================================================================
HOOK:
POV: you sell a physical product and someone confidently tells you to "just make
reels." Reels about WHAT, exactly?

CAPTION:
POV: you sell a real, hold-it-in-your-hands product, and someone tells you to
"just make reels." Cool. Reels about what?

Here's the creative loop I actually run for physical products:

→ Test ANGLES before formats. Is it "saves you time," "looks premium," or
"finally fixes X"? The angle is the bet. The format is just the wrapper.

doesn't get it, it's dead on arrival.
→ Show the product doing its job — not just sitting there looking pretty.
→ Let the data pick winners. Your favourite ad and the winning ad are rarely the
same one. (Painful. Reliable.)

Winning creative isn't an art project. It's a question you're asking the market,
fast.

Angles first or formats first — where do you start? 👇
